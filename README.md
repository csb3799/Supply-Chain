# Supply Chain Performance Analysis

An earlier R-based supply chain analysis covering product revenue, inventory, suppliers, shipping costs, and product defects.

This project is retained as a supporting case study. My newer supply chain work is more structured and is presented through the current logistics and inventory projects on my profile.

## Business questions

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which suppliers have weaker lead-time or quality results?
- Which carriers have higher shipping costs?
- Which products have higher defect rates?
- Where can operating cost be reduced without creating stock or service problems?

## Analysis flow

```text
Supply chain data
      |
Data preparation
      |
Product and revenue analysis
      |
Inventory analysis
      |
Supplier analysis
      |
Carrier and shipping cost analysis
      |
Defect analysis
      |
Recommendations
```

## Main findings

The analysis found that skincare products generated the highest revenue in the dataset. It also showed differences in shipping cost, supplier performance, and defect rates across products and suppliers.

Those findings were used to identify areas for supplier review, carrier comparison, quality control, and inventory planning.

## Business use

The analysis can support:

- supplier scorecards
- carrier cost reviews
- inventory planning
- product quality monitoring
- cost reduction work

The recommendations are based on the supplied dataset. They are not proof that changing a supplier or carrier will reduce cost. A production decision would require current contracts, service levels, capacity, and margin data.
